# The Hackathon

Welcome to the Agentic AI Hackathon! For a full month you will play the role of a team of AI experts working for AlphaInsure, an insurance company. The CTO of AlphaInsure needs your help building an AI agent following some troubled few months. She has sent you a memo detailing her needs. All the information for getting started is available in the asset list below. Have fun!
